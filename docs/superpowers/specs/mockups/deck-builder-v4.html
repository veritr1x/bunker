Compromise counter

Why this deck. Operator 6O runs the end-of-service top defense. Its strength is New Year's Belle Sarafa. Her skill drops her to 1% HP, applies Dauntless for 60 seconds (her HP can't fall below 1) and, with a dark weapon, recharges her weapon skills. Her Caged Vigor then makes her hit harder at low HP.

What doesn't work: blocking her first Dauntless. The defending side acts first, so Dauntless is already on before any Compromise lands. Compromised only stops new beneficial statuses: it doesn't remove one that's already on, and nothing a player can field removes Dauntless.

What does: outlasting her first minute, then blocking the second. Yuletide Songstress Marie revives whoever falls first. At about 60 seconds her Dauntless and our Compromise both run out. New Year's Boor Yudil's character skill charges about 2.5 times faster than hers, so his next Compromise lands before her next Dauntless. Their Rion can still cleanse it, but his cleanse removes only one or two ailments, so it may not pick Compromised. Then Phantasmal Boor Yudil finishes her, and his light attacks hit her, as a dark character, for ×1.2.Assumes the firing order players reported: defender first, then right, left, middle. The game data doesn't hold the order; the game client decides it.
