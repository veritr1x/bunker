# Official Japanese event content

Checked on 2026-11-02. The local reader is
`inputs/official-jp-event-content/index.html`; its portable archive is
`inputs/bunker-official-jp-event-content.zip`.

## Newly preserved sources

The [Square Enix Presents title index](https://www.jp.square-enix.com/presents/title/nierreincarnation.html)
links twenty surviving Japanese broadcast pages: numbered programs 0–17 and
two additional staff/writer programs. All twenty page bodies, schedules as
written, published video links and 1280 × 720 Open Graph program images were
saved. The placeholder question marks in crossover program titles remain as
published; the archive does not silently replace them with inferred titles.
Video files were not downloaded.

Two official crossover sources were also saved:

- [Dragon Quest news, October 11, 2023](https://www.dragonquest.jp/news/detail/3878/).
- [Dragon Quest X event topic, updated October 12, 2023](https://hiroba.dqx.jp/sc/topics/detail/50cf0763d8eb871776d4f28b39deb564/).

These pages distinguish the DQX event period, October 11–22, from the
Reincarnation collaboration period, October 6–November 6. Times and dates stay
in their published context and are not assigned to global game notice IDs.
The updated topic explicitly corrects an earlier letter-paper illustration;
both the news article's illustration and the topic's corrected illustration
are retained with their own source associations.

## Image scope and validation

The collection contains 44 distinct published image payloads: twenty program
images and twenty-four images from the two crossover sources. The latter
include three sharing icons, two unrelated DQ product promotions and one store
QR code. Those six files are retained as source evidence and omitted from the
reader's main artwork galleries. They are not described as recovered banners.

All 44 images were fully decoded, dimension-checked, SHA-256 recorded and
visually reviewed on two contact sheets. The reader has 22 source pages with
local artwork, attributed source links and escaped readable text. Raw response
bodies and extracted fragments retain independent hashes. The generated reader
has no embedded remote video players or original website scripts.

These are Japanese external publisher sources. No additional original in-game
notice ID or exact missing-original image path has been established. The
764-path gap remains unchanged.
